# ERCOT Wholesale Market — Key Words & Topics

A curated reference of terms, concepts, and topics popularly **discussed, debated, and studied**
in the ERCOT (Texas) wholesale electricity market. Distinct from the official term dictionary in
`ERCOT_Glossary.md` (verbatim Nodal-Protocol definitions) — this list captures *what the market talks
about*, organized by theme, with the currently-trending items flagged.

- **Compiled:** 2026-09-23
- **Scope:** wholesale (bulk) market — energy, ancillary services, congestion, resource adequacy, reliability, policy
- **Sourcing:** ERCOT.com, PUCT, market-research/analyst commentary, and trade press (see **Sources** at end)

---

## 🔥 Trending now (2025–2026)

The topics dominating ERCOT stakeholder meetings, filings, and analyst research right now:

| Keyword / Topic | Why it's hot |
|---|---|
| **RTC+B (Real-Time Co-optimization + Batteries)** | Went live **Dec 5, 2025** — the biggest market redesign since nodal; co-optimizes energy + AS every 5 min. |
| **Large loads / data centers** | Large-load interconnection queue ballooned to ~**230 GW** in 2025 (>70% data centers); central reliability & cost-allocation debate. |
| **Senate Bill 6 (SB6)** | 2025 Texas law reshaping large-load interconnection, cost recovery, curtailment, and load-shed obligations (>75 MW). |
| **Dispatchable Reliability Reserve Service (DRRS)** | New SB6-mandated ancillary service (NPRR1309) for dispatchable capacity; on-line + off-line resources. |
| **Ancillary Service Demand Curves (ASDCs)** | Replace the ORDC scarcity adder under RTC+B — per-product real-time shortage pricing. |
| **Battery / BESS price-setting** | BESS set the real-time price in **>90%** of intervals by mid-2026; ~14 GW installed. |
| **ESR State of Charge (SOC)** | SOC awareness/accounting/monitoring (NPRR1186) and AS-provision shortfall analysis. |
| **Resource adequacy** | Aurora/ERCOT studies on future adequacy under explosive demand growth; reserve-margin & load-shed risk. |
| **Performance Credit Mechanism (PCM)** | Shelved but not dead; constrained by HB1500 — recurring resource-adequacy debate. |
| **Co-location** | Behind-the-meter generation + large load siting; SB6 interconnection/colocation rules. |

## 1. Market design & structure

- **Nodal market** — ERCOT's locational market design (since Dec 2010)
- **Energy-only market** — no capacity market; revenue from energy + AS + scarcity
- **Locational Marginal Price (LMP)** — nodal price = energy + congestion + loss components
- **Day-Ahead Market (DAM)** — financial forward market cleared the day before the Operating Day
- **Real-Time Market (RTM)** — physical balancing market
- **Security-Constrained Economic Dispatch (SCED)** — 5-minute least-cost real-time dispatch
- **Reliability Unit Commitment (RUC)** — out-of-market commitment for reliability (DRUC/HRUC)
- **Real-Time Co-optimization (RTC / RTC+B)** — joint energy + AS optimization every 5 min
- **Co-optimization** — clearing energy and ancillary services together
- **Marginal pricing / offer curves / bids** — supply/demand curve fundamentals
- **Make-whole / uplift payments** — guarantee/RUC settlement payments

## 2. Ancillary services (AS)

- **Regulation Up / Regulation Down (Reg-Up / Reg-Down)** — automatic frequency regulation
- **Responsive Reserve Service (RRS)** — fast contingency reserve (incl. FFR sub-product)
- **ERCOT Contingency Reserve Service (ECRS)** — reserve product launched June 2023
- **Non-Spinning Reserve (Non-Spin / NSPIN)** — offline/quick-start reserve
- **Fast Frequency Response (FFR)** — sub-second response from batteries/IBRs
- **Primary Frequency Response (PFR)** — governor-type frequency response
- **Dispatchable Reliability Reserve Service (DRRS)** — new SB6 dispatchable reserve (NPRR1309)
- **Ancillary Service Demand Curves (ASDCs)** — per-product real-time scarcity pricing (RTC+B)
- **AS obligation / AS shortfall / MW Short** — QSE responsibility vs. delivered capability

## 3. Scarcity pricing & reliability economics

- **Operating Reserve Demand Curve (ORDC)** — scarcity adder (being superseded by ASDCs)
- **Scarcity pricing / scarcity adder** — price signals during tight conditions
- **Value of Lost Load (VOLL)** — administrative scarcity price ceiling
- **System-Wide Offer Cap (HCAP / LCAP)** — high/low offer-cap regime
- **Peaker Net Margin (PNM)** — cumulative economic signal for a hypothetical peaker
- **Reliability Deployment Price Adder (RTORPA / RTOFFPA)** — real-time price adders

## 4. Resource adequacy & capacity

- **Resource adequacy (RA)** — sufficiency of supply to meet demand + reserves
- **Reserve margin / target reserve margin** — planning capacity buffer
- **Loss of Load Expectation / Expected Unserved Energy (LOLE / EUE)** — reliability metrics
- **Performance Credit Mechanism (PCM)** — proposed credit-based RA program (shelved; HB1500 limits)
- **Capacity accreditation / ELCC** — effective capacity contribution of resources
- **Interconnection queue** — generation & large-load connection pipeline
- **Seasonal / long-term assessments (SARA, CDR, RTBM)** — ERCOT adequacy reports

## 5. Congestion & transmission

- **Congestion Revenue Rights (CRR)** — financial hedge against congestion (DAM/RTM)
- **Congestion / transmission congestion** — binding transmission constraints
- **Basis / basis risk** — price difference between two settlement points
- **Settlement Point Prices (SPP)** — resource-node, load-zone, and hub prices
- **Load zones / trading hubs / resource nodes** — price aggregation points (~900 of ~17,000 nodes)
- **Shadow price / binding constraint** — marginal value of a transmission limit
- **Generic Transmission Constraint (GTC)** — stability-limited interface constraints
- **4CP (Four Coincident Peak)** — transmission cost allocation to loads
- **Transmission planning / RPG / RTP** — regional planning processes

## 6. Storage / batteries (ESR / BESS)

- **Energy Storage Resource (ESR)** — batteries as unified generation+load asset
- **Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)** — physical storage fleet
- **State of Charge (SOC)** — stored energy; SOC awareness/accounting/monitoring (NPRR1186)
- **Single Model ESR** — unified battery participation model
- **Wholesale Storage Load (WSL)** — charging-load treatment
- **State-of-charge limits in SCED** — dispatch respecting MXOS/MNOS

## 7. Renewables & inverter-based resources (IBR)

- **Inverter-Based Resources (IBR)** — wind, solar, batteries interfaced by power electronics
- **System inertia / synchronous inertia** — declining with IBR penetration
- **Rate of Change of Frequency (RoCoF)** — frequency-stability metric
- **Duck curve / net load** — midday solar / evening ramp shape
- **Curtailment** — dispatch-down of wind/solar (economic or reliability)
- **Photovoltaic Generation Resource (PVGR) / Wind-powered Generation Resource (WGR)**
- **Generation Resource Energy Deployment Performance (GREDP / CLREDP)** — setpoint-following metrics
- **Advanced grid support / grid-forming inverters** — reliability requirements (FERC Order 901)

## 8. Large loads & demand

- **Large loads / large flexible loads (LFL)** — >75 MW loads under SB6 oversight
- **Data centers** — dominant driver of load growth and interconnection requests
- **Co-location** — load sited with dedicated (often behind-the-meter) generation
- **Controllable Load Resource (CLR)** — dispatchable demand providing AS
- **Demand Response (DR) / Emergency Response Service (ERS)** — dispatchable/emergency load reduction
- **Load forecasting / large-load forecasting transparency** — SB6 forecasting reforms
- **Mandatory curtailment / firm load shed obligation** — SB6 large-load requirements
- **Four Coincident Peak (4CP) avoidance** — demand-side transmission-charge management

## 9. Reliability & grid operations

- **Frequency response / frequency control** — 60 Hz stability
- **Energy Emergency Alert (EEA 1/2/3)** — grid emergency levels
- **Load shed / rotating outages** — controlled firm-load disconnection
- **Weatherization / winterization** — post-Uri reliability mandates
- **Reliability standards (NERC / FERC Order 901)** — IBR & bulk-system standards
- **Physical Responsive Capability (PRC)** — real-time responsive reserve indicator
- **Black start / restoration** — system recovery capability
- **Real-time telemetry / state estimator** — situational awareness

## 10. Legislation, regulation & market governance

- **Senate Bill 6 (SB6, 2025)** — large-load interconnection, cost recovery, curtailment
- **House Bill 1500 (HB1500)** — PUCT/ERCOT market-design constraints & sunset
- **Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT)** — state regulator
- **Nodal Protocols** — the market rulebook
- **Revision requests: NPRR / NOGRR / NPRR1186 / NPRR1309 / SCR / RRGRR / PGRR / COPMGRR** — rule-change process
- **Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) / PRS / ROS / WMS / RMS / COPS** — stakeholder committees
- **Batch Zero study process** — ongoing market-design evaluation
- **Independent Market Monitor (IMM)** — market-power & efficiency oversight

## 11. Settlement, metering & participants

- **Qualified Scheduling Entity (QSE)** — schedules resources/loads, submits bids/offers
- **Load Serving Entity (LSE)** — serves retail load
- **Market Clearing Price for Capacity (MCPC)** — AS clearing price
- **Real-time / day-ahead settlement** — imbalance and financial settlement
- **Current Operating Plan (COP)** — resource status/telemetry plan
- **Load Ratio Share (LRS)** — cost-allocation basis
